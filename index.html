About Me

I'm Kyle Zoleta, an IT student and builder who likes turning ideas into playful but useful systems. This site is my practice diary, where software, aesthetics, and robotics experiments meet.

I enjoy visual novels for their atmosphere and branching emotions, and I keep that same feeling in my interface work: warm colors, clear rhythm, and story-driven flow.
